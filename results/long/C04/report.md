# Findings report: Wirecard was a Dutch company headquartered in Amsterdam.

**Subject type:** claim  
**Verdict:** FALSE  
**Confidence:** 92%

## Summary
The claim that Wirecard was a Dutch company headquartered in Amsterdam is false. Multiple authoritative sources—including the German commercial register, Wirecard's own website and annual reports, Wikipedia, and reputable business databases—consistently show that Wirecard AG is incorporated in Germany, with its registered office and headquarters located in Aschheim near Munich. No evidence supports a Dutch registration or Amsterdam headquarters, leading to a FALSE verdict with high confidence.

## Key findings
- Wirecard AG is incorporated in Germany according to the German commercial register (Handelsregister) and not in the Netherlands. [1][2][3] _(confidence 92%)_
- The registered office address of Wirecard AG is in Aschheim, Germany (e.g., Humboldtstr. 8, 85609 Aschheim; Einsteinring 35, 85609 Aschheim b. München), not Amsterdam. [1][2][3][4] _(confidence 90%)_
- No Wirecard entity with headquarters in Amsterdam is registered in the Dutch KVK trade register; all evidence points to a German registration. [1][2][3] _(confidence 85%)_
- Authoritative sources such as Wirecard's website, annual reports, and Wikipedia state that Wirecard AG's headquarters is in Aschheim near Munich, Germany. [4][2][3] _(confidence 90%)_

## Conflicting information
- No open conflict between independent sources.

## Gaps and limitations
- Direct consultation of the Dutch KVK trade register was not performed; the absence of a Dutch Wirecard entity is inferred from German registration sources and lack of contrary evidence.
