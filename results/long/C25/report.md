# Findings report: Northvolt filed for bankruptcy in Sweden in March 2025.

**Subject type:** claim  
**Verdict:** TRUE  
**Confidence:** 90%

## Summary
The weight of evidence shows that Northvolt AB filed for bankruptcy in Sweden on March 12, 2025, following a Stockholm District Court decision, and announced the filing on its own website the same day. Conflicting information about an earlier February 18, 2025 date appears in an outdated company page and is outweighed by numerous timely, independent sources. Gaps remain in the availability of the exact court docket number and official registry extracts, which would further strengthen verification.

## Key findings
- The legal name of the Northvolt entity is Northvolt AB and its Swedish Companies Registration Office (Bolagsverket) registration number is 559015-8894. [1][2][3][4][5][6] _(confidence 96%)_
- Northvolt AB filed for bankruptcy in Sweden on March 12, 2025, as recorded by the Stockholm District Court. [5][7][8][9][10][6] _(confidence 90%)_
- Northvolt AB issued an official website announcement on March 12, 2025 stating it had filed for bankruptcy in Sweden. [7][8][10] _(confidence 90%)_
- An outdated Northvolt.com financing article from August 2023 incorrectly lists a bankruptcy filing date of February 18, 2025. [11] _(confidence 20%)_

## Conflicting information
- No open conflict between independent sources.
  - Assessment: Conflicting dates for the bankruptcy filing: multiple reliable sources (Reuters, Wikipedia, Eurofound, Ackordscentralen, Northvolt's own March 12 announcement) state March 12, 2025, while an outdated Northvolt.com financing article from August 2023 incorrectly lists February 18, 2025. The March 12 date is weighted more heavily due to the higher number of independent, timely sources and the explicit reference to a Stockholm District Court decision.

## Gaps and limitations
- No direct Stockholm District Court docket or case number was provided in the evidence to independently verify the bankruptcy filing.
- No extract from the Swedish Companies Registration Office (Bolagsverket) bankruptcy register or the Swedish Enforcement Authority was cited.
- No independent verification from a secondary financial regulatory filing (e.g., to the Swedish Financial Supervisory Authority) beyond the company's own announcement and news reports.
