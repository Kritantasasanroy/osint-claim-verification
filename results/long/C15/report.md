# Findings report: Nikola Corporation (electric truck maker)

**Subject type:** company  
**Verdict:** ADVERSE  
**Confidence:** 90%

## Summary
The preponderance of evidence shows that Nikola Corporation, the Delaware‑incorporated NASDAQ‑listed electric truck maker founded by Trevor Milton, has been involved in serious fraudulent conduct leading to criminal convictions, a substantial SEC settlement, and a Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing. These adverse facts support an ADVERSE verdict with high confidence.

## Key findings
- Nikola Corporation is incorporated in Delaware and trades on NASDAQ under the ticker NKLA. [1][2] _(confidence 97%)_
- Trevor Milton founded Nikola Corporation in 2014 (or 2015) and served as its CEO and executive chairman. [3][4][5] _(confidence 96%)_
- Nikola Corporation's headquarters is located in Phoenix, Arizona. [5] _(confidence 95%)_
- Trevor Milton was convicted of fraud and sentenced to four years in prison, and Nikola Corporation settled SEC charges for $125 million for defrauding investors. [6][4][5] _(confidence 95%)_
- Nikola Corporation filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy on February 19, 2025; the bankruptcy plan is now effective and the SEC received a $4 million payment in December 2025. [7][8] _(confidence 97%)_
- Nikola Corporation is currently a party to a federal securities fraud class action lawsuit. [9] _(confidence 40%)_
- No pending regulatory actions beyond the SEC settlement were identified in the available evidence. [4] _(confidence 80%)_
