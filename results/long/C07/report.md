# Findings report: Elizabeth Holmes was acquitted of all charges in her 2022 fraud trial.

**Subject type:** claim  
**Verdict:** FALSE  
**Confidence:** 94%

## Summary
The claim that Elizabeth Holmes was acquitted of all charges in her 2022 fraud trial is false. She was convicted on multiple investor‑fraud counts, acquitted only of patient‑fraud counts, sentenced to over eleven years in prison, and her conviction has been upheld on appeal.

## Key findings
- Elizabeth Holmes was not acquitted of all charges; she was convicted on multiple fraud counts in her 2022 federal trial. [1][2][3][4] _(confidence 94%)_
- The jury found Holmes guilty on one count of conspiracy to commit wire fraud and three counts of wire fraud related to investors, and not guilty on four patient‑fraud counts. [2][4][3] _(confidence 93%)_
- Holmes was sentenced to 135 months in federal prison, ordered to surrender on April 27, 2023 and to report on May 30, 2023. [4][5][6] _(confidence 92%)_
- Her appeal to overturn the conviction was denied by the Ninth Circuit, leaving the 2022 guilty verdict intact. [6][5][7] _(confidence 90%)_
