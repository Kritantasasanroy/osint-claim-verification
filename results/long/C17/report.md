**Subject type:** person

## Summary
The adverse verdict reflects Trevor Milton's criminal conviction for fraud, his subsequent presidential pardon that removed incarceration but left the conviction's legal status ambiguous, the dismissal of SEC civil actions, and the persistence of pending private civil lawsuits against him personally.

## Key findings
- Trevor Robert Milton was convicted in October 2022 of one count of securities fraud and two counts of wire fraud and sentenced in December 2023 to four years in prison, a $1 million fine, and $168 million in restitution. [1][2][3] _(confidence 96%)_
- In March 2025, President Donald Trump granted Trevor Milton a full and unconditional pardon, which vacated his prison sentence and resulted in no active incarceration record as of August 2025. [4][2][5] _(confidence 95%)_

- In September 2025, the SEC filed a joint stipulation to dismiss, with prejudice, its civil enforcement action against Trevor Milton, indicating no active SEC sanctions or civil judgments against him personally. [7] _(confidence 98%)_
- As of August 2025, private civil lawsuits filed by Nikola investors against Trevor Milton remain pending, with settlement stipulations dated August 12 and August 21, 2025. [8][9] _(confidence 62%)_
