# Findings report: Wirecard's financial statements were audited by EY (Ernst & Young).

**Subject type:** claim  
**Verdict:** TRUE  
**Confidence:** 90%

## Summary
The claim that Wirecard's financial statements were audited by EY is true with high confidence: EY served as Wirecard's auditor for the 2018 and 2019 fiscal years and remained the auditor up to the insolvency filing in June 2020, and no official records show a prior auditor change.

## Key findings
- EY was named as the auditor in Wirecard AG's auditor's report for the fiscal year ended 31 December 2019. [1][2][3] _(confidence 90%)_
- EY was named as the auditor in Wirecard AG's auditor's report for the fiscal year ended 31 December 2018. [4][5][3] _(confidence 90%)_
- Ernst & Young continued to serve as Wirecard AG's auditor up to the insolvency filing on 25 June 2020. [1][6][2][7][3] _(confidence 85%)_
- No official filings (German commercial register, BaFin announcements) indicate a change of auditor for Wirecard AG prior to its insolvency. [4][2] _(confidence 80%)_

## Conflicting information
- No open conflict between independent sources.
  - Assessment: No conflicts detected in the evidence.

## Gaps and limitations
- Direct copies of the auditor's reports for 2018 and 2019 were not provided; reliance on secondary summaries.
- No explicit BaFin announcement or commercial register entry confirming auditor appointment was located in the evidence.
- Evidence does not include the actual tender outcome or shareholder resolution for the 2019 audit, leaving a small uncertainty about whether a change was formally approved but not implemented.
