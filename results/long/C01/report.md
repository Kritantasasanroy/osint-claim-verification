# Findings report: Wirecard AG

**Subject type:** company  
**Verdict:** ADVERSE  
**Confidence:** 92%

## Summary
The evidence shows Wirecard AG was a German‑registered payment processor founded in 1999 that collapsed in June 2020 after admitting a €1.9 billion shortfall, filed for insolvency, and faced active US and German litigation. While the corporation itself has not been convicted, its former CEO was charged with fraud and the German Federal Court confirmed ongoing shareholder damage claims. These factors support an adverse verdict with high confidence.

## Key findings
- Wirecard AG is registered in the German commercial register with address Einsteinring 35, 85609 Aschheim and registration number HRB 169227 (Munich). [1][2] _(confidence 95%)_
- Wirecard AG was founded in 1999. [3] _(confidence 90%)_
- Wirecard AG filed for insolvency on 25 June 2020 after disclosing a €1.9 billion missing amount. [3][4][5][6] _(confidence 95%)_
- As of September 2020, a consolidated US class action and five German opt‑in proceedings were active against Wirecard AG. [7] _(confidence 90%)_
- The German Federal Court ruled that shareholder damage claims in the Wirecard insolvency are subordinated to general unsecured creditor claims, indicating ongoing litigation. [8] _(confidence 85%)_
- Former Wirecard AG CEO Markus Braun was charged in March 2022 with fraud, breach of trust, and accounting manipulation. [3] _(confidence 90%)_
