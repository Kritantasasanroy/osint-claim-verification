# Findings report: Trevor Milton is currently serving a prison sentence for fraud.

**Subject type:** claim  
**Verdict:** FALSE  
**Confidence:** 86%

## Summary
The claim that Trevor Milton is currently serving a prison sentence for fraud is false. He was convicted of securities and wire fraud in 2022, sentenced to four years imprisonment in 2023, but received a full and unconditional pardon from President Trump on March 28, 2025, which erased his conviction and sentence. He remained free on bail pending appeal and, as of August 2025, appears nowhere in the Federal Bureau of Prisons inmate population, indicating he is not incarcerated.

## Key findings
- Trevor Milton was convicted in October 2022 of one count of securities fraud and two counts of wire fraud in the Southern District of New York. [1][2] _(confidence 96%)_
- He was sentenced on December 18, 2023 to 48 months' imprisonment and three years of supervised release. [2][3][4] _(confidence 95%)_
- President Donald Trump granted Trevor Milton a full and unconditional pardon on March 28, 2025, officially recorded by the Department of Justice. [5][6][4] _(confidence 92%)_
- Following the pardon, Trevor Milton remained free on bail and was not incarcerated; his conviction and sentence were erased, so he is not currently serving a prison sentence. [7][8][6][9] _(confidence 86%)_
- As of August 2025, the Federal Bureau of Prisons shows no inmate record for Trevor Milton, confirming he is not incarcerated. [9][2][8] _(confidence 84%)_
