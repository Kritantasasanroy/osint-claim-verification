# Findings report: Patagonia's founder transferred ownership of the company to a trust and a nonprofit in 2022.

**Subject type:** claim  
**Verdict:** TRUE  
**Confidence:** 92%

## Summary
The evidence confirms that Patagonia's founder, Yvon Chouinard, transferred ownership of the company in 2022 to the Patagonia Purpose Trust (which holds all voting stock) and the Holdfast Collective (which holds all nonvoting stock). The transfer is documented on Patagonia's ownership page and reported by multiple news outlets. While the founder no longer holds direct voting shares, he retains practical influence via the trust. Minor gaps remain regarding the precise filing date, trust and nonprofit formation documents, and SEC disclosures.

## Key findings
- The founder of Patagonia, Inc. is Yvon Chouinard. [1][2][3][4][5] _(confidence 96%)_
- In 2022, Yvon Chouinard executed a legal transfer of ownership of Patagonia, Inc. [3][4][6][7][8][9][5] _(confidence 93%)_
- Ownership of Patagonia, Inc. was transferred to the Patagonia Purpose Trust (voting stock) and the Holdfast Collective (nonvoting stock). [10][6][8][9][5] _(confidence 92%)_
- Patagonia's ownership webpage (updated September 2022) serves as a publicly available document recording the transfer to the Patagonia Purpose Trust and Holdfast Collective. [10][6] _(confidence 85%)_
- After the 2022 transfer, the founder does not retain any voting shares; the Patagonia Purpose Trust owns all voting stock of Patagonia, Inc. [10][5] _(confidence 88%)_

## Conflicting information
- No open conflict between independent sources.
  - Assessment: Wikipedia states that after transferring voting stock to the Patagonia Purpose Trust, Yvon Chouinard retains effective control of Patagonia via the trust, whereas Patagonia's ownership page says the trust owns all voting stock and the founder does not retain any voting shares. Both sources agree the trust holds the voting stock; the disagreement concerns the founder's indirect control. We weighed the Patagonia ownership page as a primary source describing the legal structure, and interpreted Wikipedia's comment as describing practical influence, concluding that the founder does not hold direct voting shares but retains influence through the trust.

## Gaps and limitations
- The exact date of the state filing or legal registration that formalized the transfer to the Patagonia Purpose Trust and Holdfast Collective is not provided in the evidence.
- Copies of the trust agreement for the Patagonia Purpose Trust and the registration documents (e.g., IRS determination letter, state nonprofit filing) for Holdfast Collective are not available in the evidence set.
- No SEC Form 8-K or equivalent corporate filing disclosing the ownership change is cited in the evidence.
- Registration numbers or official identifiers for the Patagonia Purpose Trust and Holdfast Collective are not supplied.
