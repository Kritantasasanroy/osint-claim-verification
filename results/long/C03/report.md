**Subject type:** person

## Summary
Markus Braun, former CEO of Wirecard AG, led the company from January 2002 until his resignation in June 2020, was arrested shortly thereafter, and has remained in pre‑trial detention while awaiting trial on charges of fraud, breach of trust, and accounting manipulation filed in March 2022. He has not been convicted, and a Munich Regional Court has ordered him and other former board members to pay EUR 140 million in damages plus interest. The criminal trial commenced in 2022 and continues, but its expected conclusion, the enforcement status of the civil judgment, and any ongoing regulatory sanctions are not established by the current evidence.
