# Findings report: Northvolt filed for bankruptcy in Sweden in March 2025.

**Subject type:** claim  
**Verdict:** TRUE  
**Confidence:** 90%

## Summary
Based on a preponderance of reliable sources—including company press releases, financial news outlets, and Eurofound’s restructuring event sheet—Northvolt AB filed for bankruptcy in Sweden on 12 March 2025. A single conflicting source suggesting an 18 February 2025 filing was deemed weak and outweighed by stronger evidence. The legal name of the Swedish entity is Northvolt AB, though its exact Bolagsverket registration number remains unverified in the current evidence, and the original court filing document has not been accessed.

## Key findings
- The legal name of the Swedish entity that operates as the parent of the Northvolt group is Northvolt AB. [1] _(confidence 90%)_
- Northvolt AB filed for bankruptcy in Sweden on 12 March 2025. [2][3][4][5][6][7][8][9] _(confidence 90%)_
- The bankruptcy filing was announced via a press release from Northvolt North America and confirmed by Northvolt's own website and Eurofound's restructuring event sheet. [2][5][6] _(confidence 85%)_
- Northvolt Labs AB (registration number 559144-2891) entered bankruptcy proceedings on 12 March 2025. [3] _(confidence 80%)_
- Northvolt Revolt AB (registration number 559237-8060) is a Swedish subsidiary of Northvolt. [3] _(confidence 80%)_
- The bankruptcy filing did not include Northvolt Germany or Northvolt North America, according to the company's press release. [4] _(confidence 70%)_

## Conflicting information
- No open conflict between independent sources.
  - Assessment: A northvolt.com article (strategic review) states a bankruptcy filing date of 18 February 2025, whereas multiple other sources (press releases, financial news, Eurofound) state the filing occurred on 12 March 2025. The conflicting date was weighed against the stronger evidence: the March 12 claim is supported by eight direct sources with weights ranging from 0.40 to 0.95, while the February 18 claim is a single weak refutation with weight 0.12. Consequently, the March 12 date was accepted as the correct filing date.

## Gaps and limitations
- The exact registration number of Northvolt AB as recorded with the Swedish Companies Registration Office (Bolagsverket) is not present in the provided evidence.
- Direct access to the official bankruptcy filing document from the Stockholm District Court (to confirm the filing date and entity) is not available in the evidence set.
- Any subsequent updates or amendments to the bankruptcy proceedings after the March 2025 filing are not covered by the supplied sources.
