# Findings report: Wirecard's financial statements were audited by EY (Ernst & Young).

**Subject type:** claim  
**Verdict:** TRUE  
**Confidence:** 88%

## Summary
The available evidence indicates that Ernst & Young acted as Wirecard's auditor for the fiscal years 2018 and 2019, with no indication of another audit firm during the 2015‑2020 period, supporting the claim that Wirecard's financial statements were audited by EY.
