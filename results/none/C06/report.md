# Findings report: Elizabeth Holmes, founder of Theranos

**Subject type:** person  
**Verdict:** ADVERSE  
**Confidence:** 96%

## Summary
Elizabeth Holmes, founder and former CEO of Theranos, has an adverse record marked by a criminal fraud conviction, SEC civil settlement with financial penalties and industry bans, and a substantial civil judgment ordering her to pay $125 million to Rupert Murdoch. While she appealed her conviction in April 2023, the appeal remains unresolved, and no personal bankruptcy or additional major civil actions are documented in the available evidence.

## Key findings
- Elizabeth Holmes founded Theranos and served as its Chief Executive Officer and Chairman. [1][2][3][4][5] _(confidence 99%)_
- Elizabeth Holmes was criminally convicted of fraud related to Theranos. [1][4][3][5][6] _(confidence 98%)_
- Elizabeth Holmes settled SEC civil charges, paying a $500,000 fine, returning shares, relinquishing voting control, and being barred from officer/director roles for ten years. [2][7][8][6][3][5] _(confidence 97%)_
- Elizabeth Holmes was ordered to pay Rupert Murdoch $125 million in a civil judgment. [9][7][3] _(confidence 95%)_
- As of the latest available information, Elizabeth Holmes appealed her conviction in April 2023 and the appeal remains pending with no final ruling evidenced. [6] _(confidence 80%)_
