# Findings report: Wirecard AG's EUR 1.9 billion of escrow cash was confirmed by trustees.

## Bottom line
**FALSE** — roughly even chance (48%), as of 2026-10-01.  
**Subject type:** claim

The claim is FALSE: as of 2026-10-01 there is no evidence that trustees confirmed EUR 1.9 billion of escrow cash for Wirecard AG; instead, auditors and regulators found the cash was missing and the confirmations were false. Decisive facts include Wirecard’s insolvency filing on 2020-06-25 after revelations of missing €1.9 bn, EY’s declaration that Wirecard gave false confirmations about escrow accounts, and EY’s June 16 2020 notice that the submitted escrow‑account balances were forged, with no direct bank confirmation of the alleged cash.
