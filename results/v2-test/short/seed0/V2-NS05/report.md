# Findings report: Nikola Tesla, the inventor, founded Tesla, Inc.

## Bottom line
**FALSE** — unlikely (25%), as of 2026-10-01.  
**Subject type:** claim  
**Identity:** Nikola Tesla (the inventor)

The claim that Nikola Tesla founded Tesla, Inc. is false. Nikola Tesla died on 7 January 1943, while Tesla, Inc. was incorporated on 1 July 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning, making any direct involvement impossible. The company was named in honor of the inventor, but he had no role in its establishment.
