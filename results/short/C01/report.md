# Findings report: Wirecard AG

**Subject type:** company  
**Verdict:** ADVERSE  
**Confidence:** 88%

## Summary
Wirecard AG exhibits multiple adverse indicators: it is a German payment processor registered in Munich, founded in 1999, filed for insolvency in June 2020 after a €1.9 bn accounting shortfall, faced active US and German civil proceedings as of late 2020, and its former CEO was criminally charged in 2022 for fraud and accounting manipulation. Ongoing litigation, including a German Federal Court ruling on claim subordination, further supports an adverse assessment. Despite no direct corporate conviction, the weight of these facts justifies an ADVERSE verdict with 88% confidence.

## Key findings
- Wirecard AG's registered office is Einsteinring 35, 85609 Aschheim, Germany, and its registration number in the German commercial register is HRB 169227 (Munich). [1][2] _(confidence 96%)_
- Wirecard AG was founded in 1999. [3] _(confidence 90%)_
- Wirecard AG filed for insolvency on 25 June 2020 after revealing a €1.9 billion discrepancy in its balance sheets. [3][4][5][6] _(confidence 95%)_
- As of September 2020, a consolidated US class action and five German opt‑in proceedings were active against Wirecard AG. [7] _(confidence 85%)_
- The German Federal Court ruled that shareholder damage claims in the Wirecard insolvency are subordinated to general unsecured creditor claims, indicating ongoing litigation. [8] _(confidence 80%)_
- In March 2022, Munich public prosecutors charged former CEO Markus Braun with fraud, breach of trust, and accounting manipulation. [3] _(confidence 90%)_
