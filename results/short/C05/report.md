# Findings report: Theranos

**Subject type:** company  
**Verdict:** ADVERSE  
**Confidence:** 95%

## Summary
The evidence shows that Theranos, Inc. was founded in 2003 by Elizabeth Holmes, became the subject of SEC fraud charges and a settlement, faced DOJ indictments leading to convictions of Holmes and Balwani, and received a CMS warning letter about laboratory deficiencies. These adverse legal and regulatory actions support an ADVERSE verdict with high confidence.

## Key findings
- Theranos was founded in 2003 by Elizabeth Holmes. [1][2][3][4] _(confidence 95%)_
- In March 2018, the SEC charged Theranos, Elizabeth Holmes, and Ramesh Balwani with fraud, and Theranos and Holmes agreed to settle those charges. [5][6] _(confidence 95%)_
- The DOJ indicted Elizabeth Holmes and Ramesh Balwani in June 2018 for fraud; Holmes was later convicted on four fraud counts and sentenced to over 11 years imprisonment, while Balwani was convicted on twelve counts of wire fraud and conspiracy. [7][8][9][3][4] _(confidence 90%)_
- Theranos and Elizabeth Holmes settled the SEC fraud charges, agreeing to disgorge funds and pay penalties. [6][5] _(confidence 95%)_
- CMS sent a warning letter to Theranos in July 2016 citing laboratory deficiencies, preceding any subsequent loss of CLIA certification or Medicare/Medicaid sanctions. [7] _(confidence 70%)_

## Gaps and limitations
