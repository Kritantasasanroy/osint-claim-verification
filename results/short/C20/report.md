**Subject type:** company

## Summary
The ADVERSE verdict (85% confidence) is based on documented enforcement actions against Patagonia, Inc. within the last ten years: an EEOC settlement in 2019, a Seattle Office for Labour Standards fine and employee compensation in 2022 for violating the Secure Scheduling Ordinance, and an NLRB charge of unfair labor practices in 2020. These findings demonstrate a pattern of regulatory and labor‑law adverse conduct, supporting the adverse assessment despite the lack of sanctions listings or bankruptcy events.
